Feed

Newest first. Four kinds of news only. Investments: foreign money going into Indian companies or assets. Exits: a foreign investor selling an Indian stake (fully or partly), a foreign company closing its Indian operations, or a fund investing in India being liquidated. Fund launches: funds set up outside India (offshore or GIFT City) to invest in India, including their registrations and closes. Jurisdiction moves: funds investing in India changing domicile. Several outlets covering the same deal show as one row; Reports is how many articles covered it.
