Methodology and sources

India Capital Tracker collects public information about foreign money moving into and out of India. This page says where each part comes from and how much weight it can bear.

Source grades

GradeMeaningHow it is used
APublished by a regulator, depository or exchange (NSDL, NSE, BSE, SEBI, RBI, CCIL, IFSCA, DPIIT).Shown as data. Totals are built from these.
CReported in the press or other secondary sources. Unverified.Shown as a lead with a link to the publisher. Never added into any total.

Refresh

The collector runs hourly. Each source publishes on its own schedule, so every panel shows the date its data runs to. Where a source is late, the last good figure stays visible with its date.

Limits you should know about

  • Automated classification of news can be wrong. Check the original before relying on any grade C item.
  • Calendar-year and fiscal-year totals differ; each figure says which it is.
  • Rule summaries are plain-English orientation, not the official text. Follow the link to the official document.
