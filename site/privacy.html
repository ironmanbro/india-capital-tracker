Privacy notice

Last updated 17 November 2026

This notice explains what the site collects, why, and your choices. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules under it.

1. Who is responsible

Kishan Singhania, in a personal capacity, decides how data on this site is used (the "data fiduciary" under the DPDP Act). Contact: see the line at the foot of this page.

2. What is collected

  • Anyone visiting: a random visitor ID kept in your browser's local storage (not a cookie), used to count visitors and page views. It contains no name, email or device identity. The time of your last visit and cached exchange rates are also stored in your browser. The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Signed-in users: your email address, or your name and email from Google or LinkedIn if you use them to sign in, and a login record managed by our authentication provider. While signed in, the site records which pages you open and what you search, with a short device type, to improve the site and keep your preferences. Notes and watchlists you save are stored against your account.
  • Technical logs: our hosting and data providers process your IP address and request details as part of delivering the site and protecting it from abuse.

The site does not ask for, and you should not enter, government IDs, financial account details, or confidential information of your employer or clients.

3. Why, and on what basis

To run the site, count usage, keep it secure, save your preferences and notes, and understand which features are useful. Signed-in tracking is based on your consent, given with a tick box when you sign up. Basic visit counting and security logging are for the legitimate operation and protection of the site.

4. Who handles the data

  • Supabase (database and sign-in).
  • Cloudflare (hosting, caching, network security).
  • Google Fonts (typefaces), which receives your IP address when fonts load.
  • Google and LinkedIn, only if you choose them to sign in.

These providers may process data outside India. Your data is not sold, rented or used for advertising. It is shared only with these providers to run the site, or when required by law.

5. How long it is kept

Visit records are kept in aggregate for site statistics. Account data, activity records and notes are kept while your account exists and deleted within 30 days of a deletion request, except where law requires otherwise.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Clear the visitor ID any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Use the site without signing in. Nothing beyond the items above is collected.
  • Ask to access, correct or delete your data, to withdraw consent, or to raise a complaint, using the contact line below. Withdrawing consent means your account and its tracking are removed.
  • If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to tables is controlled by row-level rules, so signed-in users can read only their own notes and activity. No system is perfectly secure, and the site cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your data occurs, you will be told as the law requires.

8. Children

The site is for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

9. Changes

This notice may change. The date above shows the latest version.
