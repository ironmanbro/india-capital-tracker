    How to read this

    • Held is the aggregate foreign portfolio investment in the company as a percentage of its paid-up capital. Cap is the permissible limit that applies to it.
    • Room is the cap minus held, in percentage points; room in shares is NSDL's figure for how many more shares FPIs can still buy.
    • A company on the breach list is already above its limit, so room is negative and no share count is given.
    • Star a company to follow it: you will see it on your Watchlist when it enters, leaves or changes list.
    • This is a record of what NSDL publishes, not a view on any company. It does not say whether a price will rise or fall.
