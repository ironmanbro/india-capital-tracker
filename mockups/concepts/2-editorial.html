Foreign investors have put ₹1.5 lakh crore into Indian markets this month, reversing October’s exit

November’s net buying is almost entirely equity, as large blocks changed hands in healthcare and building materials.

By the Ledger desk · Data: NSDL, NSE · Updated 26-Nov-26 14:08 UTC

Net inflows over the last five sessions came to ₹41,208 crore, taking the November total to ₹1,53,624 crore. October saw a net outflow of ₹24,475 crore. Equity accounts for ₹1,45,308 crore of the month’s buying, with debt adding ₹7,614 crore.
